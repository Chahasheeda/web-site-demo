Admission Policy

Marks Eligibility Criteria & Merit Tiers

Our online portal enforces a strict single minimum passing cutoff of 60.0% aggregate marks in your qualifying 12th Standard / Senior Secondary examination.

Below 60.0% Not Eligible

Below Minimum Cutoff

Candidates scoring below 60.0% aggregate do not meet the minimum eligibility criteria. The online admission form automatically locks submission when aggregate marks are below 60%.

  • Form submission is blocked automatically
  • Candidate may apply after improvement exam results
60.0% – 74.9% Eligible • General Merit

Standard Admission Eligibility

Meets the mandatory 60% minimum passing cutoff. Unlocks admission application for all undergraduate programs (B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, B.A).

  • Full eligibility across all 4 UG degrees
  • Eligible for standard counseling & hostel allotment
75.0% – 89.9% Eligible • 25% Scholarship

First Class with Distinction

Exceeds eligibility cutoff with distinction. Automatically qualifies for the Dean's Merit Scholarship (25% tuition fee waiver) in the first academic year.

  • Priority specialization allocation
  • 25% automatic reduction on annual tuition
90.0% – 100% Eligible • 50% Scholarship

Chancellor's Honors List

Outstanding academic merit. Qualifies for direct spot admission and a 50% Chancellor's Scholarship renewable every year.

  • Direct admission without waitlisting
  • 50% tuition waiver + research mentorship grant
