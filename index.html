Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding eligibility, course fees, and duration discounts.

What is the eligibility criteria for Direct Admission? +

Students scoring 70% and above in their previous academic examinations qualify for Direct Admission without any mandatory entrance test or counseling session.

What happens if my marks are between 50% and 69%? +

Students scoring between 50% and 69% are eligible for admission with counselling recommended to help choose the best-suited course track according to their background.

How are duration discounts calculated? +

When you enroll for a 3-month package, you receive an automatic 5% discount. When enrolling for 6 or more months, you receive a 10% discount on the total course fee.

Can I get counsellor support if my marks are below 50%? +

Yes! Students scoring below 50% can connect directly with our academic advisor via the WhatsApp button or admissions desk for personalized foundation training.
